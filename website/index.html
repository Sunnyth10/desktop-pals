Questions, answered

Which computers and operating systems are supported?

DeskPals runs natively on macOS 12 Monterey or later (dedicated builds for Apple Silicon arm64 at ~88 MB and Intel x64 at ~91 MB) and Windows 10 (build 1903+) or Windows 11 (64-bit at ~84 MB). Storage requirement is lightweight: only ~200 MB of free disk space. Multi-monitor setups and Retina / HiDPI displays are fully supported.

Will it slow my computer down?

Barely. CPU stays under 3% while active and under 1% when sleeping. Memory usage is under 150 MB for a single companion, and under 300 MB when running all 5. Frame rate runs smoothly at 60 FPS when active and dials down to ~10 FPS idle to conserve power.

Will companions block my mouse clicks?

Never. DeskPals uses a per-pixel click-through transparent overlay (NSWindow on macOS and transparent frameless window on Windows). Empty space passes clicks directly to your apps, code editor, and browser—only the companion’s own pixels catch clicks.

Can I have more than one pal active?

Yes! You can toggle Cat, Fox, Robot, Web Hero, and Ghost individually, or have all 5 roam your screen at the same time. They can even react to and notice each other.

Does it collect my data or record my screen?

Never. DeskPals is 100% offline-first and private. There is zero telemetry, no cloud analytics, and no accounts. Your mouse position is polled only locally for movement physics, and optional macOS Accessibility is used purely in local memory to check the active app title during Focus mode—no window contents, keystrokes, or screen recordings are ever captured.

Is DeskPals free? What is the license?

DeskPals is 100% free and open source under the MIT License. You can use it, contribute to it, or fork it freely on GitHub without any fees, paywalls, or subscriptions.
